GLANCE: Global-to-Local Agentic Navigation for Cascade Evidence

in LLM-based Fake News Verification

Paper (arXiv) Code & Dataset

Abstract

Social media fake news verification requires models to reason over both textual content and complex social propagation structures. Although large language models (LLMs) have shown strong semantic reasoning ability, directly feeding the entire social cascade into LLMs often suffers from noisy context, excessive token consumption, and limited context windows.

To address these challenges, we propose GLANCE, a Global-to-Local Agentic Navigation framework. GLANCE deploys multiple lightweight agents to navigate over the global cascade from complementary perspectives. A budget-aware aggregation module constructs a compact local evidence graph for final LLM truthfulness reasoning.

Extensive experiments demonstrate that GLANCE achieves strong Macro-F1 performance while reducing input token consumption by over 73% compared with global-cascade prompting.
